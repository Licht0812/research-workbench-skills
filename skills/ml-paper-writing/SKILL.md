# ML Paper Writing

Own the requested manuscript text when this workflow is selected. Other writing guidance may advise, but one primary writer maintains the canonical manuscript.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the requested passage or full-paper scope, contribution, available evidence, source format, and current manuscript path.
2. Map each substantive claim to actual method details, results, or verified sources; keep missing evidence distinguishable from completed findings.
3. Draft or revise the requested text around problem, insight, mechanism, evidence, and implication; preserve numbers, equations, citation keys, and authorized scope.
4. Check claim consistency and citations. For LaTeX use the host's available editor/compiler and inspect relevant output when compilation is part of the request.

Preserve the supplied findings and source format; distinguish evidence gaps from requested prose edits.

Read [the workflow reference](references/workflow.md) when implementation decisions or validation details are needed.

## Deliverable

The requested revised prose or manuscript first, plus relevant validation and concrete evidence gaps.
